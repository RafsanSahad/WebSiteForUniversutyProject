How Frame Interpolation Works

Frame interpolation creates smooth transitions by generating new frames between existing ones. This technique can convert a 30fps video to 60fps, making motion appear more fluid.

Higher frame rates result in smoother motion. Common target rates are 60fps for standard content and 120fps for slow-motion effects.
